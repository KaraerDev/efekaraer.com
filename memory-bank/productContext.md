Warm paper, ink, vermilion, exaggerated condensed headlines and exhibit numbering. The contrast is elaborate presentation with almost indifferent, assured copy. Do not explain the contrast on the site.

Visitors should immediately recognize Efe, explore the 15-photo collection, discover jokes without losing usability, and reach real gaming/social profiles. The archive is a destination rather than filler under a generic portfolio hero.

Keep factual content and joke UI distinct. The Master rank is explicitly archival, FIDE 3500 is explicitly a joke, new anecdotes should come from Efe. No developer biography, fake job history, guessed social links, or unverified live stats.

Efe does not introduce himself or need visitors' approval. The site treats his significance as established, without explicitly stating that premise. Dry, restrained Turkish replaces warm invitations and repeated “ben” headlines. The site must still glorify Efe, not turn him into a defensive loser or outsider's parody. Functional copy stays clear. Read voiceAndCopy.md before writing public text.

Enrichment should feel discovered: preparation marginalia, a listening snapshot, historical game evidence, and a single footer-triggered visual interruption. Most of the site stays restrained. Authentic Efe wording is an explicit exception when supplied, not a pretext for generic internet slang. See lore.md for actual content provenance and omitted ideas.
